Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

KSSR English Go! is made for primary school pupils, so we keep it simple: the app does not collect any personal data.

  • No accounts, no sign-in. You never give us your name, email or any other details.
  • Everything stays on your device. Your answers, scores, bookmarks and settings are saved only on your iPhone or iPad. They are not sent to us or to anyone else.
  • No tracking, no ads, no analytics. The app does not use any third-party tracking, advertising or analytics tools, and it does not connect to the internet on its own.
  • Purchases. In-app purchases are processed by Apple. We do not see or store your payment details. Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.
  • Face ID. Face ID is only used to confirm that you want to reset your statistics. The check happens entirely on your device; the app never receives your face data.

Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

If this policy changes, we will update this page. Questions? Email tanchonghow1997@gmail.com.
